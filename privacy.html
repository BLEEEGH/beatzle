Beatzle privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Beatzle is a music block puzzle game made by BLEEEGH Studio. This page explains what data the game uses.

What the game stores

Your progress (scores, Notes, unlocked items and settings) is saved on your own device. We do not run any servers and never receive your progress or any personal information.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android you can choose to sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, Google keeps a copy of your progress in your Google account so it carries over to other phones, and records your achievements. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Photos you pick

Some runners and the snake can wear a photo you choose from your phone. The photo is shrunk and saved inside the game on your device only. It is never uploaded or shared.

Ads

The game can show optional rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google (AdMob in the Android app, AdSense on the web). Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general location to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The Android app uses internet access for ads and Play Games, and vibration for haptic feedback. When you save a song as an alarm or ringtone, it is written to your phone's Alarms folder; older Android versions (9 and below) ask for storage permission for this. The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location, and only opens a photo when you pick one yourself.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the game saved on your phone, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it. To delete your cloud copy and achievements, remove Beatzle from your Google Play Games account settings, or email us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
